Cloudbet: proposed 2027 structure

Incumbent global betting partner

Cloudbet holds rights across every event but cannot legally activate in most of the markets where those events land.

The fix Strip out what they cannot use, replace it with inventory that travels, reprice against what is deliverable.
1

Retitle to reflect scope

Naming sets the hierarchy

  • Official International Betting Partner of PFL — international feed and out-of-territory digital only.
  • Regional partners take Official Betting Partner of PFL in [market].
  • No global betting exclusive. Exclusivity becomes territorial.
2

Event assets: geo-targeted virtual overlay only

No physical branding out of territory

  • Cage mat, apron and LED by digital board replacement on the international feed.
  • Domestic feed carries the regional partner. Neither brand appears in the other's market.
3

Year-round global content, not event-tagged

The core of their reach

Owned series across the calendar, tied to no specific event, so nothing hangs on a fight they cannot legally promote.

  • Fighter predictions series.
  • Behind-the-scenes and fighter access content.
  • Archive and highlights licence.
  • VIP hospitality across the slate, including events outside their territory.
  • Ambassadors, rest of world — shoots, appearances, social and odds segments. Travels where signage cannot, so this is the strongest asset in a package with no in-venue presence. Booked against agreed days, allocated to avoid clashes.
4

In-territory uplift

Full domestic package where they are licensed

  • Events inside their territory list: all physical branding, in-venue, VIP hospitality and ticketing, domestic social and digital.
  • Priced on a rate card, so expansion is a purchase rather than a renegotiation.
  • Gives them a direct reason to widen their licensing footprint.
5

Netflix blockbuster build-up, as an upweight

Paid add-on, not part of the base

  • Rest-of-world rights to fight week, weigh-in, press conference, fighter content and owned social across the four Netflix events.
  • Domestic slice sold separately to the US partner.
  • The cheapest lever for holding the fee up once the territory list narrows.

Confirm Netflix advertising standards on gambling, and whether they accept an operator unlicensed in the US, before pricing this.

6

Slim down the territory list

Reserved markets go to licensed partners

  • Remove all regulated European markets — UK, France, Germany, Spain, Italy, Netherlands.
  • Remove the United States and Australia.
  • These form the reserved market schedule, sold to licensed regional partners.
  • PFL can add markets to the schedule on notice as events are announced.
7

First refusal on new territories

Keeps the relationship growing

As Cloudbet acquires licences, they take first refusal on that market at the pre-agreed rate, moving it onto the in-territory uplift.

Alt

The all-in exclusive

Sole betting operator across the full slate, at a premium

If Cloudbet would rather hold everything than take a narrowed territory, we sell it — priced as the closure of every other betting sale on the slate, not as an uplift on the current fee.

  • Exclusive betting operator in cage and ring at every event.
  • Broadcast branding on every feed where it can lawfully be carried.
  • All social and digital betting inventory, no regional operator alongside.
  • Full ambassador and VIP hospitality allocation.
  • Netflix build-up included in the base.
  • First refusal on the 2028 slate.
  • The floor is the forecast regional and standard package value combined, plus an exclusivity premium. Set before we open, not negotiated toward.
  • Single season, 2028 option. A multi-year all-in locks us out of the regional market with no relationships built.
  • Activation minimums in the markets they can serve, with a release if unmet.
  • Regulatory sign-off first. Confirm with legal exactly where Cloudbet can sit on the canvas, ring and broadcast in each market, and get it approved before we go to market.
